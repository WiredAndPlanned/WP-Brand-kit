One family of calm, warm planners.

This kit shows what makes a WiredAndPlanned planner look and sound like us: the Cairn mark, the ink, the color families, the type and the words. Everything in it is built from the 17 planners. Tap any color to copy it.

The 17 planners, each with its own app icon.

01 · The brand

Who we are

WiredAndPlanned makes digital planners that open in any browser and sit on the Home Screen like an app. They're calm, warm and easy to use, and most come alive with soft, moving artwork. Every one shares the same mark, ink and type.

The name
WiredAndPlanned

One word, with capital W, A and P. Use it in sentences, titles and sign-offs.

The small wordmark
WIREDANDPLANNED

JetBrains Mono in capitals with wide spacing. It sits under a planner's name, never on its own as a logo.

Page titles

03 · Color · The brand's own

Ink and paper

The brand's own colors: warm ink on light, warm paper. They start from the Minimalist planner, with lighter paper in light mode and lighter text in dark mode. Use them for the brand itself, for all text, and for anything that is not one single planner.

The brand accent is Ink itself: #26211C, pressed #000000 on light; #F7F3EC, pressed #FFFFFF on dark.

04 · Color · Black and white
